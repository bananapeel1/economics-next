  KEY: increase poverty, as the poor spend most of their income on food | others: reduce poverty, as the rich now pay more for their food ; leave poverty unchanged, as total tax revenue is the same ; reduce poverty, as lower income tax raises everyone's wages

## 5 Causes of Inequality
DIAGRAM: Why Wealth Concentrates | Assets grow 6% a year when returns are reinvested / Wages grow 2% a year / Both start from the same value / The gap widens faster every year | views: Compounding
SVG: Assets compound faster than wages · Value (start = 1) · Years · 1 · 4 · 10 · 20 · 30 · ×5.74 · ×1.81 · Assets, 6% a year · Wages, 2% a year · After 30 years: assets ×5.74, wages ×1.81 · Owners of wealth pull away from earners.
Q: Which is most likely to explain a lasting gap in income per head between two countries?
  KEY: less capital and fewer skills per worker in the poorer one | others: a higher poverty line in the richer one ; a larger population in the richer one ; a lower Gini coefficient in the poorer one
