PACKET 36 BLOCKING DEFECT — diagram legibility on 390px phones

STATUS: Verify B found a gate-blocking defect that prevents this packet from passing.

THE DEFECT
===========
Every diagram in the staged section renders with text at 7.1–9.3 CSS px on a 390px viewport,
against body copy of 14–17px. The diagrams are roughly half the readable size of surrounding prose.

WORST CASE: Step 17 (What the Business Holds and Owes)
- Statement of financial position table: figures like "Inventory $240,000", "Trade payables $180,000"
- Ratio workings: "Current ratio = $400,000 ÷ $250,000 = 1.60:1"
- All rendered at 8.54px — the smallest text on the page, and the one a student must read numbers from

MEASUREMENT
============
Diagram component: 440-unit viewBox width
Learn column at 390px: 313 CSS px available
Scale factor: 0.711
Authored font sizes: 10–13px
Rendered on screen: 10–13px × 0.711 = 7.1–9.3px

Six diagrams, all affected:
| step | diagram | rendered range | affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Profit Ladder | 8.54–9.25px | 26 of 26 labels |
| 12 | Measuring Profitability | 7.11–8.54px | 21 of 21 labels |
| 17 | Statement & working capital cycle | 8.54–10.67px | 60 of 60 labels |
| 22 | Measuring & Improving Liquidity | 7.11–8.54px | 20 of 20 labels |
| 29 | How Businesses Fail | 8.54–9.25px | 21 of 21 labels |

WHY CHECKS MISSED IT
=====================
1. No validator rule checks rendered font size (only authored fontSize)
2. No table check exists for text nodes inside SVG (only HTML <table>)
3. No layout check catches content that fits the viewport because it has been scaled down
4. The measurement that catches it is: authored-fontSize × (rendered-width ÷ viewBox-width)

THE ESCAPE HATCH FAILS
=======================
Tapping a diagram opens an enlarge modal redrawn at 789–858px with text at 21.5–23.3px — readable,
but wider than 390px. Result: the student gets one of two things:
- Complete and unreadable (original scale)
- Readable and requiring two-axis panning (in modal)

There is no fit-to-width that is both readable and complete on the 390px phone this packet was built for.
