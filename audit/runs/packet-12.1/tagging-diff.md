## What the disagreements say

The two passes disagree in one direction more than the other, and it is worth naming which.
Pass 2 is lexical: it cannot tag a question that examines a leaf in different words, so every
question whose wording avoids the specification's own vocabulary comes back empty from it.
That is why 4 of the 19 questions carry no agreed tag and stay untagged rather than being credited
to a leaf on one pass's word. An untagged question is visible to `npm run spec-coverage`; a
wrongly tagged one is not, and would inflate the coverage number permanently.
