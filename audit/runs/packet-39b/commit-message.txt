Second half of trade-global-economy. Packet 39a built sub-topics 1-3 and carried the live
"Protectionism and the WTO" block forward; 39b builds sub-topics 4 and 5 and replaces it. Together
the two halves take 4.3.2 from 2 blocks to 10 blocks and 51 subsections, the largest section in the
programme, for the 46 leaves the specification gives it.

The hazard that governed the packet: loadBundle() selects the `data` column, which is the LIVE
published row. 39a is staged and not published, so its four blocks exist only in `draft`. A runner
copied from 39a would have rebuilt on top of the two-block live section and destroyed 39a's work
with every check passing, because every check would have been measuring a bundle that was internally
consistent and missing half the section. 39b builds from 39a's committed snapshot and uses the draft
as a witness. The first version of that check asserted the draft equalled the snapshot and built
from the draft, which is correct exactly once: the moment it staged, the next run failed its own
first check with 168 cascading problems.

One market drawn three ways is what makes accuracy-01 demonstrable rather than asserted. A $10
tariff on a $20 world price: consumers lose $900, producers gain $300, the government takes $400,
and $200 goes to nobody. The live text said the loss exists because producer gains are smaller than
consumer losses plus revenue, which is true of every tariff ever levied and identifies nothing; the
loss exists because $900 exceeds $300 PLUS $400. A 40-unit quota gives the identical price and loss
with the $400 as rent; a $10 subsidy leaves consumers at the world price and so loses $100, half.
